# Goal one-pager

Once a goal is agreed, it gets forwarded, quoted, and put on slides — usually
without the reasoning that made it a good goal. This page is the version that
travels: short enough to read, complete enough that nobody has to guess.

Write it after the [outcome hypothesis](/templates/outcome-hypothesis) or
[OKR set](/templates/okr-set) is settled. One page. If it needs two, the goal is
probably two goals.

## The template

```
GOAL
  <One sentence. An outcome, not a deliverable.>

FOR WHOM
  <Who is better off, and how they'd describe the difference in their words.>

WHY NOW
  <What makes this the thing this cycle. One or two lines.>

HOW WE'LL KNOW
  Primary measure: <measure> from <baseline> to <target> by <date>
  First reading:   <earlier, smaller signal and when we expect it>

WHAT WE WON'T TRADE AWAY
  <guardrail> · <guardrail>

WHAT WE'RE NOT DOING
  <The adjacent, tempting work this goal explicitly excludes.>

IF WE'RE WRONG
  <What we'd see, what we'd do about it, and why that's an acceptable outcome.>

CHALLENGE THIS
  <Name> is the owner. <How and by when to disagree — a channel, a meeting,
  a person. Make it concrete; "reach out any time" means nobody will.>
```

## Worked example

> **GOAL** — New starters in customer operations are productive in their first
> fortnight instead of their second month.
>
> **FOR WHOM** — New joiners, who currently describe week one as "shadowing
> someone who's too busy to explain", and their team leads, who lose a day a
> week to onboarding admin.
>
> **WHY NOW** — We're hiring 40 people into ops this year, more than the last
> three years combined. The current path doesn't scale and we already know it.
>
> **HOW WE'LL KNOW** — Days to first independently-handled case, from 26 to 10,
> for the next two cohorts. *First reading:* cohort of six starting week 3 — we
> expect to see under 18 days even from a rough first version.
>
> **WHAT WE WON'T TRADE AWAY** — Quality-assurance pass rate on new-starter
> cases stays at or above the team average. Nobody handles a vulnerable-customer
> case unsupervised, regardless of the number.
>
> **WHAT WE'RE NOT DOING** — Rebuilding the LMS. Rewriting the knowledge base.
> Both are real problems and neither is this quarter's.
>
> **IF WE'RE WRONG** — If cohort one is still at 20+ days, the constraint is
> systems access, not training, and we'd move to the access problem. That's a
> useful answer and costs us three weeks to get.
>
> **CHALLENGE THIS** — Priya owns it. Bring disagreement to the ops leads
> fortnightly, or to her directly before the 14th — after that we're committed
> for the cycle and will revisit at the review.

## Why the unusual boxes

- **"What we're not doing"** is the box that makes a goal a decision. Without it,
  every good adjacent idea quietly becomes in-scope.
- **"If we're wrong"** written down in advance is what makes a goal safe to miss.
  Agreed before the fact, a miss is a finding; agreed after, it's an excuse.
- **"Challenge this"** with a name, a channel and a date is the difference
  between psychological safety as a value and as a mechanism.
